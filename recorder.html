🎙️ Syllable recorder

Step 1 · Microphone check
Click the button, allow the microphone, then say something. The green bar should move.
How to record (read this first)
  1. Find a quiet room. Hold the microphone (or phone) about a hand's width from your mouth. Chrome will ask for permission to use the microphone: click Allow.
  2. Press Space (or the red button), say the syllable shown clearly, then press Space again to stop. A little pause before and after is fine; I trim it automatically.
  3. Say it the way you want students to hear it. If you like a slightly longer vowel (baaa), do that for every syllable so they all match.
  4. Press P to play it back. Not happy? Press Space and record it again (it replaces the old one). When it's good, press → for the next one.
  5. Your recordings are kept in this browser, so you can stop and continue later. When you're done (or whenever you like), click Save all to a folder and pick a folder. Then tell me which folder.

Start with the first group, the "a" syllables (21 recordings). They are used by the easiest levels, so they matter the most. The other vowels can come later.

Step 3 · Build the new set and put it in the game

Record as many as you like. Any syllable you did not record keeps the current built-in voice. Building trims the silence and evens out the loudness for you.
