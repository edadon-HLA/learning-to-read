SpellQuest: Privacy

SpellQuest has two games: the Hebrew reading game ("SpellQuest AlphaBet") and the math game ("SpellQuest: Math Adventure"). Both are made by Dr. Dadon Eldar for HLA Hebrew Public Charter School and work the same way, so this page covers both. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

We keep as little as possible. This page says what is saved, where, and who can see it.

Students at school

A student plays with a school code and a hero number. Their progress is saved on the device and in the school's own Google Sheet, in the school's own Google account. The student's real name and class are kept only there, for the teacher. The game service, and the people who make the game, do not receive student names.

Students never sign in with Google. If a parent wants a child's data deleted, they ask the teacher or the school owner, who can remove the student.

Play at home

A parent signs up once, with an e-mail code or with Google. The e-mail address is not saved. With the e-mail code, only a scrambled fingerprint of the address is kept, to count how many people play. With Google, the address is only used to check the sign-in and is not kept at all. The hero and the progress stay on the device only.

Play offline

Nothing is sent anywhere. The hero stays on the device and is not counted.

Sign in with Google (teachers, school owners, the game's administrator)

Adults can choose "Sign in with Google" instead of an e-mailed code. Google confirms who the person is and gives the game their verified e-mail address, and nothing else: no password, no contacts, no files, no other Google data. The game checks the address against the school's staff list (or the administrator's address) and then starts a sign-in that lasts a few hours. The address is not saved by the game when a Google account is used by a home player.

An account that is not on the staff list is turned away without saying who is on the list. Sign-out ends the sign-in.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking across other sites, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No accounts for children.

Questions or deletion requests

For a student at school, ask your teacher or school owner. For anything else, write to learningtoread.app@gmail.com.
